# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A guide to how OctoAcme initiates, plans, executes, releases, and improves projects.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative project lifecycle. Teams validate the need and desired outcomes during initiation, align stakeholders and success measures, then plan scope, ownership, milestones, risks, and dependencies. During execution, teams track work on a project board, deliver and review small increments, test changes, and communicate progress and blockers. Releases follow readiness checks, deployment verification, and rollback planning. After sprints, releases, or incidents, teams capture lessons and track improvement actions. Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, schedules, and communication; Product Managers prioritize outcomes and measure impact; developers, QA, and stakeholders contribute to implementation, validation, and decisions.
