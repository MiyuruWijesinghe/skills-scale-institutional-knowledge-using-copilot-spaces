# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer or User Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers ensure that the solution is usable, intuitive, and grounded in customer needs. They translate user insights into product flows, prototypes, and validation feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and synthesize insights
- Design interfaces, user journeys, and interaction patterns
- Validate design choices with usability testing and stakeholder feedback
- Partner with Product Managers on problem framing and acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers to ensure feasible, testable implementations

### Goals
- Improve usability, clarity, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework by aligning design with real user needs early
- Support informed product decisions with evidence

### Typical Communication
- Discovery interviews and research summaries
- Design critiques and prototype reviews
- Feedback loops during sprint demos and QA validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to define outcomes, user value, and experiment goals.
- Collaborates with Developers to validate technical feasibility and ensure implementation matches the intended flow.
- Supports QA/Testing by clarifying edge cases, usability expectations, and acceptance scenarios.
- Keeps Project Managers informed of design dependencies, milestones, and risks that may affect delivery.

---

## Technical Lead or Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, system design, and technical quality across delivery. They help balance business needs with platform constraints and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and key integration decisions
- Review system design, dependencies, and implementation trade-offs
- Support risk identification and mitigation for technical complexity
- Guide code quality, patterns, and standards across the team
- Coordinate technical sequencing with Product and Project leads

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and reliable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and platform risk
- Align implementation decisions with the roadmap and business priorities

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and design discussions
- Technical dependency tracking and risk updates
- Guidance during planning, sprint reviews, and incident triage

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on trade-offs between scope, technical complexity, and delivery timelines.
- Works with Project Managers to surface dependencies, sequencing risks, and milestone impacts.
- Guides Developers on technical standards, patterns, and review expectations.
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on testability, observability, and release readiness.

---

## Delivery/Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Delivery or Engineering Leads align engineering execution with team capacity, priorities, and quality expectations. They help the team ship consistently while managing dependencies and delivery rhythms.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate engineering work across squads or functions
- Help prioritize backlog items and resolve delivery bottlenecks
- Monitor execution against milestones and team capacity
- Support cross-team collaboration and issue escalation
- Partner with Project Managers on status, risks, and release readiness

### Goals
- Improve team throughput and predictability
- Keep delivery on track while preserving quality and maintainability
- Surface issues early before they become blockers

### Typical Communication
- Delivery standups and cross-functional coordination meetings
- Capacity and dependency discussions with project stakeholders
- Status updates tied to sprint and release milestones

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to sequence features based on strategic priority and delivery constraints.
- Supports Project Managers in tracking milestones, risks, and communication needs.
- Coaches Developers on execution quality and dependency management.
- Aligns with QA/Testing to confirm readiness criteria and release confidence.

---

## Business Owner or Sponsor

### Role Summary
Business Owners or Sponsors define business value, authorize funding and priorities, and help resolve strategic trade-offs. They provide accountability for outcomes and strategic alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business outcomes, success metrics, and strategic priorities
- Provide decisions on trade-offs, approvals, and escalations
- Validate that the investment delivers expected value
- Support stakeholder alignment and sponsorship across the organization
- Help resolve competing priorities and business constraints

### Goals
- Ensure the project remains aligned with business goals
- Make timely decisions that reduce delays and ambiguity
- Maximize value realization from delivery investments

### Typical Communication
- Steering updates, milestone reviews, and executive briefings
- Decision logs and approval checkpoints
- escalation discussions for risks, trade-offs, or route changes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm priority, outcome expectations, and business context.
- Partners with Project Managers on schedule, milestones, dependencies, and stakeholder communication.
- Provides direction to the team when key trade-offs or scope decisions are required.
- Engages Developers and QA/Testing only where business impacts, release timing, or operational readiness need alignment.

---

## Operations/SRE or Release Manager

### Role Summary
Operations, SRE, and Release Managers ensure the solution is stable, observable, and ready for production deployment. They help teams manage operational risk and delivery confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define release readiness and deployment safeguards
- Monitor system health, reliability, and operational alerts
- Support rollback planning, incident response, and post-deploy verification
- Partner with engineering on observability, resilience, and support readiness
- Coordinate communications around incidents, deployments, and uptime impacts

### Goals
- Minimize service disruption and deployment risk
- Improve production readiness and operational resilience
- Support a reliable handoff from delivery to runtime support

### Typical Communication
- Release checks, deployment reviews, and incident updates
- Operational readiness and risk signals during milestone reviews
- Post-release follow-up and support coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA/Testing to confirm release readiness, smoke testing, and rollback plans.
- Supports Project Managers with deployment windows, stakeholder notices, and risk communication.
- Aligns with Product Managers on launch timing, user impact, and support needs.
- Escalates business or service-impacting issues through the same communication and escalation paths defined in the project process docs.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Teams can adapt these roles to match their structure; one person may cover multiple responsibilities, and ownership should remain clearly defined for every key decision and deliverable.
